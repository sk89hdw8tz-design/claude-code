## METHOD NOTE THAT CHANGES HOW ROUND-1 NUMBERS MUST BE READ

The brief says v3.2 sits at v2 + (278,278) with "per-unit rigid deviations of a few px".
**That is not true of v3.2.** Template-matching v3.2 patches into v2 (`qc32_align.locoff`,
420-px template, ±260 search) gives per-unit residuals of **-85 to +182 px**, and they vary
*within* a unit (unit 2: +59 at its centre, +182 near Avenue D at 18th–19th) because the shear
pivot moved. Every v3.2-vs-v2 comparison below is therefore made on **locally re-aligned**
crops, with the match score quoted. Any round-2 measurement done at a fixed +278 offset —
including several of my own first-pass runs — produces false "content loss".

VERDICT: FAIL

- **Fix 5 half-succeeded and created a worse defect.** The 1,160-px black rule is gone (solid
  black in the 19th corridor 27,868 → **167 px**, backing band 69 → **0 rows**, sheet 7's
  Bake Ho./OVENS. block tops fully restored) — but the new cut destroyed the **"19TH ST." label
  and the 8" W. PIPE main**: 24,278 of v2's 28,292 ink px in that window (86 %) are gone,
  replaced by blank roadway paper, more ink lost than the 18,110 px recovered.
- **Fix 6 failed both of its acceptance criteria and re-created the round-1 defect elsewhere.**
  Two "25TH ST. OR BATH AV." prints remain (now 533 px apart instead of 240), sheet 11's
  "SEE SHEET No.4" is cut away, and unit 4's scan edge is now a **823 x 34 px solid black rule at
  x 11939–12762, y 20104–20137** — 14,530 solid-black px where v2 had zero, covering 3,792 px of
  v2 map content.
- **Fix 7 is a clean, verified success.** "AV. H OR WILLIAMS E." is whole on continuous paper
  (v2 beheaded 57 % of every glyph); the gutter is **52 px at x 13687–13738, y 16267–20099** —
  16 px narrower than v2's and moved 19 px west, clear of the label, zero ink inside it.
- **Three NEW duplicated street labels.** 20TH ST. (offset -45,+25), 23RD OR TREMONT at G–I
  (offset -396,+404) and 26TH ST. (offset -20,+58) are each printed twice in v3.2 and once in v2;
  near-duplicate display-label pairs 18 in v3.2 vs 13 in v2. All three are the mirror of the 19th
  failure — cuts moved far enough to admit both sheets' edge print.
- **The junk caps did NOT open paper-fill holes**, and several margins improved. Canvas-wide there
  are only four new flat-fill regions (three benign, one the disclosed Avenue H gutter); an
  aligned 19-corridor sweep found no lost map ink other than §1b and two dropped scale-bar rulers;
  backing-white bands are now zero in all 19 corridors. Avenue E x 22nd is unchanged. **But the
  27th-at-Avenue-D jog grew to +164 px** (v3 +112, v2 +107), 26 % over the disclosed 130 px limit.
