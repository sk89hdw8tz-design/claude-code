## Measurement-method correction carried over from round 1 (read first)

Round 1 measured "paper" with a **loose** mask (`luma 195..246, max-min <= 45`). That mask
admits **pink brick fill** (BGR ~186,189,225 → max-min 39). Sheet 9 has the highest pink
coverage of any unit — **42.4 %** of its area (`hue_gain_report_v32.json`, next is unit 7 at
26.4 %) — so its loose-mask "paper" median is dominated by brick, not paper. Both tables are
reported below; **the strict mask (`luma 200..248, max-min <= 26`, near-neutral only) is the
correct one** and is what the seam-step, gap-fill and tile measurements use.

Round 1's *cast* finding is unaffected: v3 read unit 9 at **G > R** (209,234,229), and pink
contamination can only push R **up** — so v3's green push was real. What was an artefact was
round 1's claim that unit 9's *paper* sat far off the edition target; on the strict mask it
now does not.
