# Storm series 3 of 3 — "Raising the city"

**Subject:** They lifted the whole town
**Preview:** 2,000 buildings jacked up. Seventeen feet of sand.

Galveston did not move. It built a three-mile seawall, then raised the grade
of the entire city behind it, dredging sand from the ship channel and pumping
it under buildings lifted on jackscrews. Residents walked to work on raised
catwalks for years. St. Patrick's Church, 3,000 tons, went up five feet with
services held inside.

The 1912 map is that city. Same lots as 1899, new ground under them.

Hung together, the pair tells the whole story.
