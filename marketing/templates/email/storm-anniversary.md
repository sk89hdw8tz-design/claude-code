# Storm anniversary (send Sept 8 annually)

**Subject:** 126 years ago tonight
**Preview:** No sale. Just the map.

On this night in 1900 the storm came ashore. We're not running a promotion
today. We're sharing the 1899 map, which is the last complete picture of
the city that stood that morning.

[The 1899 map]

If you'd like to read more, the Rosenberg Library's Galveston and Texas
History Center holds the survivor accounts.
