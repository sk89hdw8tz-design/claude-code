# Storm series 2 of 3 — "September 8, 1900"

**Subject:** The night of September 8
**Preview:** The deadliest natural disaster in American history.

The storm came ashore on a Saturday evening. The surge was fifteen feet on an
island whose highest point was under nine. By morning roughly a third of the
city was gone and at least 6,000 people were dead; many estimates run higher.

On the 1899 map, everything south of about Avenue N and east of the
cemeteries was in the destruction zone. Trace it with your finger. That's
what the map is for.
