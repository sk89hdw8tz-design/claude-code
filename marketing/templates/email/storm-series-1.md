# Storm series 1 of 3 — "The city on the 1899 map"

**Subject:** What was standing in 1899
**Preview:** 37,000 people, the busiest port west of New Orleans.

In 1899 Galveston was the richest city in Texas per capita. Cotton went out
through the wharves on the north side of the island; everything else came
in. The Strand was called the Wall Street of the Southwest.

Look at the 1899 map and find:
- The wharves along the bay, pier after pier.
- Beach Hotel site, already burned in 1898, the lot still drawn.
- Ursuline Academy, which would shelter a thousand people the next year.
