# Playbook

## Positioning (what we are selling)

Not a map. A framed piece of Galveston's story: the last full survey of the
city before the 1900 Storm (1899), and the raised, rebuilt city after (1912).
Buyers are people with a Galveston connection: residents, BOI ("born on the
island"), second-home owners, people who honeymooned or grew up there, and
gift-givers for those people. Secondary: interior designers, hotels, offices
on the Strand, Texas history buffs.

## Channels, in order of expected payback

1. **Meta / Instagram (paid + organic).** Interest targeting: Galveston,
   Texas history, Moody Gardens, Pleasure Pier, Strand, UTMB alumni. Creative
   is the storm before/after story. Best ROI channel for a visual, local,
   emotional product.
2. **Google Search.** Exact-match intent: "galveston map", "old galveston map",
   "galveston 1900 storm map", "galveston wall art". Low volume, high intent.
3. **Pinterest.** "Coastal decor", "historic map wall art". Long tail, cheap.
4. **Email.** Free. Capture with a "Story of the Storm" mini-series opt-in,
   not a discount popup. Discounts cheapen a $795 print.
5. **Local partnerships (no ad spend).** Galveston Historical Foundation,
   Rosenberg Library gift shop, Strand galleries, Hotel Galvez / Tremont
   House gift shops, Galveston Bookshop. Consignment or wholesale at 50%.
6. **Etsy** as a second storefront for discovery. Historic map prints sell
   there constantly; it is the search engine for this category.
